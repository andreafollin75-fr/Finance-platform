## Definition

A tariff is a tax on goods coming into a country. It is usually a percentage of the value of the goods. The importer pays it to the customs authorities, and part or all of the cost is often passed on to customers through higher prices.

Governments use tariffs to protect domestic industries, to raise revenue, or as a bargaining tool in trade negotiations. A **trade war** is when countries respond to each other's tariffs with tariffs of their own.

## Example

*Hypothetical example.* A 10% tariff applies to an imported machine worth $50,000. The importer pays $5,000 to customs, and may raise the price it charges its own customers.

## Why it matters

- Tariffs can raise prices for consumers and companies, adding to inflation.
- They can hurt exporters in the countries targeted, and the profits of companies that depend on imported parts.
- Announcements about tariffs can move stock markets, currencies and commodity prices very quickly.
