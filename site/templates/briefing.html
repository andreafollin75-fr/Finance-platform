How stories are ranked

Similar headlines from different sources are first grouped into one story. Each story then gets a score:

  • Coverage: 3 points for each source reporting the story (up to 5 sources). A story many outlets cover is usually more important.
  • Freshness: up to 5 points, halved every {{ rules.half_life }} hours.
  • Theme: +{{ rules.theme_bonus.macro }} for macro-economics and central banks, +{{ rules.theme_bonus.markets }} for markets.
  • Official source: +{{ rules.official_bonus }} if a central bank, regulator or statistics office published it.

At most {{ rules.per_source }} stories led by the same source are shown, so one feed cannot fill the whole briefing. This ranking is mechanical: it does not judge what matters for your own situation.
